# ORDER. Already right. The sheets are lettered A B C D E F G H I - this book
# uses I, where the petrol books skip it - and the export runs in that order.
# The CENTER CONSOLE sheet is the one exception in that its header carries no
# letter at all, but it sits between B and D, so C is not in doubt.

# NAMES. Three differ from the export, two of them just ampersands:
#
#     WHEELS & TIRES                 export: WHEELS AND TIRES
#     BLADE MOTOR & CUTTING EQUIPMENT   export: BLADE MOTOR AND CUTTING ...
#
# CENTER CONSOLE is the third and the only judgement call. The export says
# CONSOLE; the drawing says CENTER CONSOL, missing the E. Everywhere else in
# these files the printed name wins, because the technician is holding the
# printed page - but that rule is there to stop us tidying away real
# differences in wording, and a dropped letter is not one. Printing CONSOL on
# the tab would read as our typo, not Husqvarna's, and the missing E does not
# stop anyone matching the tab to the page. So: CENTER from the drawing, the E
# put back.
