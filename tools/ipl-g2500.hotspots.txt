# top of the sheet: "*1: OPS ONLY".
#
# So the reader saw "20b*1" as one word that is no callout, and the four
# OPS-only parts got no hotspot. Placed on the number itself, from the word
# boxes in the text layer (page 2, 595x842 pt): "20b*1" at x 376.3-402.4,
# so "20b" is its first three of five characters, centred at x 384.1.

1 add 21b 67.67 41.98
1 add 21c 67.67 43.17
#

# have opened the cylinder assembly. Removed; the real callout 1 keeps its own.
