# ハウジング - so unlike the other Zenoah books there is no English on the sheet

# actually wrong are changed. A "-" means "keep what the portal says".

# 4  CARBURATOR is a typo in the portal. The sheet is the carburettor itself
#    (gaskets, diaphragms, metering lever); Fig.5 is how it mounts with the air
#    filter, which is why both mention it.
#

#    optional parts - and the sheet does hold the bars and chains: four bar
#    numbers, two saw chains, a bar cover and a protector. Nobody hunting a
#    guide bar or a chain would open a tab called "Accessories".
