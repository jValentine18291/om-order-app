# 125B - the gasket kit's seven phantom callouts.
#
#     <figure> add|unlisted|drop  <key> <x%> <y%>
#
# THE PROBLEM
# Ref 35 is the Gasket Kit, and the export gives it EIGHT coordinate boxes -
# one for every gasket and seal the kit contains, scattered across the whole
# engine sheet. The drawing prints "35" exactly ONCE, on the boxed "Gasket Kit"
# legend at the bottom. The other seven are places where a gasket happens to
# sit, not places where the number 35 appears.
#
# Two of them landed straight on top of the printed 30s - the crank seals -
# which is how this was caught: ipl-check-hotspots.py refuses overlaps, and
# reported "keys 30 and 35 share one number" twice. Both were checked against
# the drawing and both print 30, not 35.
#
# The remaining five sit on ink but on no printed number at all. Those are
# quieter and worse: nothing marks them, so a technician panning the sheet can
# land on one and be told a seal is a gasket kit.
#
# So all seven go and the legend one stays. What is left is what the draughtsman
# actually wrote.
#
# WHY NOT FIX THE IMPORTER
# Because a part legitimately called out in several places is normal and common
# - 29 and 30 on this very sheet are each printed twice, and the 541RB has a
# screw called out three times. There is no rule separating "the same part in
# four places" from "one part whose kit contents were mapped", except reading
# the drawing. This is what the override file is for.
