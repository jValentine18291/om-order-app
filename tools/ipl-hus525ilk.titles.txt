# 525iLK sheet titles, in the order the export lists them (A to F already).

# One name differs from the portal, and it is only the ampersand: the drawing
# sets SHAFT & HANDLE where the export writes SHAFT AND HANDLE. The other five
# agree exactly.
#
# TWO SHEETS WITH "HANDLE" IN THE NAME, and they are different ends of the
# machine. B, SHAFT & HANDLE, is the shaft, the coupling and the loop handle
# that clamps round it. D, HANDLE, is the rear grip with the trigger and the
# throttle lockout. Nothing can be done about that from here - both names are
# printed - but it is the thing to know before tapping one.
#
# THE COMBI COUPLING is what makes this a K. B carries two tube assemblies and
# a clamping sleeve because the shaft comes apart to take other attachments,
# which is also why the BEVEL GEAR sheet stands on its own as A rather than
# being part of a cutting sheet: the gear head is a fitting, not a fixture.
#
# US VARIANTS on the bevel gear. Keys 14, 15 and 16 each list two part numbers
# - one plain, one marked "For US only" - so a callout there opens two rows.
# The remarks carry the distinction; the drawing shows one screw.
