# Titles as printed on the 531RB's own drawings, read off the sheets.
# Each heads itself "531 RB / <title>" beside its section letter, and eleven of
# them carry a market in the opposite corner.
#
# THE MARKET IS THE POINT OF THIS FILE
# Eight of the twenty-five sheets come in an Asia version and a South Korea
# version of the SAME assembly - gear, tube & shaft, handlebar, throttle
# controls, accessories - and the portal files both under one name. So the app
# showed "GEAR (1 of 2)" and "GEAR (2 of 2)" with nothing to say which was
# which, and the two sheets list different part numbers.
#
# OM are in Singapore, which is Asia. A South Korea sheet does not apply to
# their machines at all, and LA is Latin America. Putting the market in the tab
# is the difference between ordering the right gearcase and the wrong one.
#
# The starters are the same story under different names: J1 STARTER is general
# and J2 EASY STARTER is South Korea only. They share 5 part numbers out of 15
# and 16, so they are genuinely different starters.
#
# Naming the sheets apart also drops the "(n of 2)" suffix, because each sheet
# now says for itself which one it is.
#
