# the bottom - and the export lists only 11. The refs step 32, 34, so from the
# data alone 33 reads like a retired number, and 15 is genuinely missing the
# same way and genuinely is not printed. 33 is printed, in the same bold
# numeral as every other callout, with a leader line to its own blade.
#
# Marked unlisted, so a technician who sees two blades on the page and needs
# the other one is told the book lists no part for it, rather than tapping a
# blade that does nothing.
#

1 unlisted 33 24.7 96.4

# TWO OTHER THINGS ABOUT THIS SHEET, neither of them fixable from here.
#
# REF 38 IS LISTED WITH NO POSITION. It is BRACKET 531208043, "Brake cable
# holder, For 970617501" - one product variant's part - and the export gives
# it no coordinates because the drawing does not print it. It stays in the
# parts list where it can be found and ordered; it simply cannot be tapped.
# This is the one case the importer reports by itself, and it did.
#
# THE ARTWORK HAS NO TITLE BLOCK, and the little text it has is clipped. The
# only line on the sheet reads "Engine HONDA GCV160E A7E1", and even that is
# cut - the E of Engine is missing its left stroke. That is Husqvarna's own
# file, not our downscaling: the original they serve is 1351px wide and is
# trimmed the same way. So there is no printed name to follow here and no
# section letter, and the tab keeps the export's COMPLETE PRODUCT. The 54
# prints PRODUCT COMPLETE on its page and its tab says so; these two lawn
# mowers disagree because their drawings do, not because we were careless.
#
# The engine line is worth knowing on its own: this book has no engine sheet
# at all. A GX 560 needing carburettor or starter parts needs Honda's GCV160E
# list, which is not in this app.
