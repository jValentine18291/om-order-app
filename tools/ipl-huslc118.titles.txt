# LC 118 sheet title. One sheet, no section letter.
#
# PRODUCT COMPLETE, where the export says COMPLETE PRODUCT - the same way
# round as the Exclusive 54, and the opposite of the GX 560, whose artwork has
# no title block at all to follow. Three lawn mowers, three answers.
#
# (2022 ON) is the other half of the printed heading. The drawing heads itself
# "LC 118 - 2022-", which is Husqvarna's way of saying this book starts at the
# 2022 machine and has no end date yet. It goes on the tab because this book
# has only one tab: there is nowhere else in the app for it to be seen, and a
# technician holding an older LC 118 should know before they order that this
# is not their book.
#
# THE SERIAL SPLIT INSIDE IT is carried by the export for once, in the
# remarks, so it needs nothing here. Three callouts change at S/N 20234450015
# / 20234450016 - the ENGINE (1), the CUTTING DISC (21) and the KEY (22) - and
# tapping any of them shows both rows with their own serial note. Worth
# knowing that the engine is one of them: a 22mm crank up to that serial and a
# 25mm from it, which is not a difference anyone should discover on the bench.
#
# THE ENGINE IS A SINGLE PART HERE. 599912201/02, HS139A, listed as one line.
# There is no engine sheet in this book, so an LC 118 wanting carburettor or
# starter parts needs Husqvarna's HS139A list, which is not in this app - the
# same gap the GX 560 has with its Honda.
